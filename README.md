## Document AI for Digital Humanities and Preservation (Main Focus)

My primary research area is historical document analysis. I have worked on document analysis and low-resource language processing for about 15 years. My academic training includes a PhD focused on AI for document analysis, and my research has received several academic awards. I build datasets that bring together historical documents in multiple languages and scripts from across Asia. My work focuses on layout analysis, text recognition, and visual question answering for complex documents and low-resource collections.

## Cybersecurity and Robotics (Personal Interests)

Alongside my research, I work on cybersecurity and the security of robotic systems. Between 2009 and 2015, I won awards in national and international cybersecurity competitions, including ASEAN events. I later shared a team win in Europe. I also earned **CEH**, **OSCP**, and **CPTS** certifications. I no longer compete. As an independent consultant, I help private companies assess the security of large language model (LLM) applications and enterprise systems.
